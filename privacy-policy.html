Lore — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Lore ("the app") is developed by ndstudio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, used for authentication (sign up / log in) via Supabase.
  • Character and quiz data: your quiz answers, generated character attributes, quest progress, and domain scores, used to run the app's core features.
  • Feedback: if you submit feedback in-app, we collect the message, category, and basic device/app diagnostic info (OS version, device model, app version) to help us respond.

How we use your data

Your quiz answers and character data are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate personalized character content, quests, and narrative feedback. This data is used only to generate your in-app content and is not used by Anthropic to train models under their API terms.

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles authentication.
  • Anthropic — processes quiz/character data to generate in-app content.
  • Resend — delivers feedback emails submitted through the app.

Data retention and deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data (character, quests, quiz results, profile) at any time from Settings → Delete account inside the app. This action is immediate and irreversible.

Children's privacy

Lore is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
